English

Every core feature of Lumamark runs offline on this PC: editing, preview, export and batching need no connection.

We do not collect your photos or templates.

There is no self-hosted server and no telemetry.

Purchases and licensing

Lumamark contacts Microsoft Store only when you actively buy the unlock, restore a purchase or refresh the license.

Payments and purchase records are handled by Microsoft. Lumamark does not receive or store payment details.
